The one thing that caught my attention in cell biology class 
was how complex a cell is no matter how small it is. The working 
function of plasma membrane with different types and how it functions.
It’s not just a barrier but a selective membrane having different functions.
I used to think only diffusion and osmosis were the only ways how
cell used to consume and withdraw things within itself.
One of such myth buster for me was the active transport systems
which use atp to transport substances against the concentration gradient.

Other such topic that grabbed my attention was the cell cycle.
The fact that cell has to go through phases like G1 , S , G2 and M to 
produce two daughter cells and if it doesn’t produce new cells it is 
suspended in a phase named G0. The phase S was the one I will 
prefer as my fav as it is where the Dna replicates and chromosomes
separation occurs properly. These different checkpoints for
Cell division ensures the cell to be divided. These phases reduce
the chances of error during cell division.

My last remark to cell biology classes will be that it 
taught me about cell alot . It requires more than mugging
up the topics to understand the cell.
